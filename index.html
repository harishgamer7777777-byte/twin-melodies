About the Chapter

Twin Melodies is a story about Shruti Sharma, a young violinist who is interested in exploring Indo-Western fusion music.

Her musical interests create a difference of opinion between her and her father, Guru Nabin Sharma, who strongly values classical musical traditions.

Through music, family conversations and understanding, the story explores the relationship between tradition, personal choice and creativity.

How Does Fusion Music Feel?

Fusion music can feel like two different musical worlds having a conversation. Each instrument brings its own personality, rhythm and sound.

When the violin, tabla, flute and keyboard come together, their different musical voices can create an exciting and harmonious experience.

It can make us feel energetic, peaceful, curious and inspired at the same time.

What is Fusion Music?

Fusion music combines different musical styles, instruments or traditions into one performance. It allows musicians to experiment while appreciating the beauty of different musical cultures.
